2

Build and sign offline

This step happens on your offline device, not here.

On your offline device, build the transaction using the nonce, gas price and gas limit from step 1, then sign it. Most offline builders show the signed transaction as a QR code. See XinFin’s guide to Send Offline if you’re setting this up for the first time.

3

Scan and broadcast

Scan the signed transaction’s QR code, or paste it. Check the summary, then broadcast it to the network.

Things to know

Nothing secret goes in here

Your address and the signed transaction are public anyway: they end up on chain. Your private key stays on the offline device and is never needed here.

Check before you broadcast

Broadcasting can’t be undone. The summary shows the recipient, amount and fee read from the signed transaction itself, so you can confirm it matches what you meant to send.

Add it to your home screen

On iPhone, tap Share then “Add to Home Screen”. On Android, open the browser menu and choose “Add to Home screen”. It opens like an app with the XDC icon.

Camera not working?

Allow camera access for this site in your browser settings (on iPhone: Settings, then Safari, then Camera). You can always paste the signed transaction instead.
